FAQ

Before you send anything.

What counts as one integration?

One user-visible AI feature or one bounded machine-to-machine flow, with a single declared trust boundary. Multiple independent systems need a narrower or separate scope.

Is this a penetration test or certification?

No. It is a bounded engineering preflight, not compliance certification, legal advice or an unrestricted security test. Any behavior testing requires an authorized safe environment and written cases.

What should I include in the first email?

A public URL or redacted description, the decision you need to make, and your target date. Do not include secrets, credentials, personal data, private prompts, wallet signatures or undisclosed vulnerabilities.

Can an agent buy this?

Yes, if it can identify the represented buyer, establish authority over the target, agree scope in writing and settle 35 USDC on Base. The catalog is also available as JSON and plain text.
